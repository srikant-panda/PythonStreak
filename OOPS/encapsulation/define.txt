Encpsukation means hide internal details and restrict direct access to some of the object's components, which can prevent the accidental modification of data. It is one of the fundamental principles of object-oriented programming (OOP) that helps to achieve data hiding and abstraction. Encapsulation allows us to bundle the data (attributes) and methods (functions) that operate on the data into a single unit called a class, and it provides a way to control access to the data through access modifiers (like private, protected, and public).
